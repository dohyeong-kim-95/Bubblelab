SCHEDULE (two steps = one full cycle over 24 cells, 12 cells per step, the second step is the first step with the legs swapped):
cell 1 = contact, LEFT leg in front | 2-3 = settling toward down | 4 = down | 5-6 = rising | 7 = passing (right leg swinging past) | 8-9 = rising toward up | 10 = up | 11-12 = right leg reaching forward, right hoof still IN THE AIR above the floor line
cell 13 = contact, RIGHT leg in front | 14-15 | 16 = down | 17-18 | 19 = passing (left leg swinging past) | 20-21 | 22 = up | 23 = left leg reaching forward | 24 = left hoof a little above the floor, about to land — the moment JUST BEFORE cell 1, not a copy of cell 1.
Cells 13-24 repeat the body/head/arm motion of cells 1-12 exactly, with only the leg roles swapped. The widest stride appears ONLY in cells 1 and 13.
